    Chairing

    I have chaired the following events over the years:

    • Area Chair for the 40th AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI);
    • I co-chaired the Demo Track for AAAI 2025;
    • I was Area Chair for Coordination, Organizations, Institutions, Norms, and Ethics for the 24th International Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems (AAMAS);
    • I was co-Chair for the Doctoral Consortium of the 35th International Conference on Automated Planning and Scheduling (ICAPS);
    • I was Area Chair for the 28th European Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ECAI);
    • I was Area Chair for Coordination, Organizations, Institutions, and Norms for AAMAS 2022;
    • I was Area Chair for the 27th European Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ECAI);
    • I was co-chair of the Demo Tracks for AAMAS 2021;
    • I co-chaired the 2017 Workshop on Coordination, Organizations, Institutions and Norms held together with AAMAS 2017 in São Paulo, Brazil;
    • I co-chaired the Porto Alegre local chapter edition of Brainhack since 2014;
    • I was chair of the AAAI Symposium on Proactive Assistant Agents;
    • I was area chair for Planning and Scheduling for the 2014 IBERAMIA;
    • I co-chaired the 5th International Workshop on Collaborative Agents (CARE 2014).
